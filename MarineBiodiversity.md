## Time-At-Depth Biodiversity Patterns for Sharks
To better understand intrataxa interactions between shark species, we calculated weighted time-at-depth metrics for sharks of the North Pacific. To do this, we collected 283 existing biotelemetry records from 119 studies with time-at-depth summarizations for 1133 individual sharks across 35 taxa. We then trained a hurdle Ensemble Random Forest model to predict time-at-depth for each species based on depth ranges reported by the IUCN. From there, taxonomic, phylogenetic, and functional trait biodiversity metrics were calculated. A comparison was made between metrics using just depth range against metrics calculated with the additional time-at-depth data. This research found that time-at-depth information can have drastic impacts on biodiversity estimates. [This research was published in the journal Ecopraphy and can be accessed here.](doi.org/10.1111/ecog.07249)​
