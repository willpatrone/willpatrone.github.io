## Inclusion and Student Success

Alongside a handful of professors who work closely with the UW Marine Biology department, we examined how student success was impacted by various levels of participation in the [GEODUC program](https://environment.uw.edu/students/future-undergrads/prepare/marine-geoduc/). The GEODUC program is a three-quarter-long program for transfer students to gain hands-on exposure to geoscience research and support the pursuit of careers in this field. Alongside my collaborators, we ran mixed effects models to estimate student success based on their level of interaction with the program. This work is currently in progress.
